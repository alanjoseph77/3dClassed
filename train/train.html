How to drive your train

  1. Your duty: drive from the first station to the last, stop at every station on the duty slip and keep to the timetable.
  2. Controls: ▲ Power more speed (press again for more, up to 8) · ▼ Brake slow down (Brake 1 gentle to Brake 6 strong) · 0 coast · EB emergency stop · Horn. Keyboard: ↑/W, ↓/S, 0, E, H.
  3. Starting: look at the signal ahead of the train. red = wait. When it turns green, press ▲ Power 3 or 4 times.
  4. Speed: obey the round speed boards and the limit shown on screen: 110 on the line, 60 near a station, 30 at the platform, 45 leaving it, 60 where men are working. The speed bar turns red if you are too fast.
  5. Signals: yellow home signal = the next station is a stop, get ready to brake. Never pass a red signal.
  6. Where to stop: stop the front of the engine at the yellow STOP board at the end of the platform (there is a yellow-black plate between the rails). When the message says BRAKE now, set the brake number it shows. The bar at the bottom counts the last 300 m.
  7. At the station: wait while passengers board. When the starter signal turns green, power up and go to the next station.
  8. Camera: drag the screen to turn around the train, pinch or scroll to zoom, double tap to reset. View switches chase, driver's cab and side views.

The yellow box at the top always tells you what to do next.
