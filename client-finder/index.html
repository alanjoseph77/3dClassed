Where to find businesses that need this

  • Google Maps: search "dentist near me", "salon", "physiotherapy", "tuition centre" in your area. Open each result: lots of reviews, a phone number, open late = busy = good lead. Look at "popular times" to find the very busy ones.
  • Instagram: search the same words with your city. Businesses that post often but answer comments slowly are your best leads.
  • Walk in: 10 shops on one street in an afternoon beats 100 online messages. Ask for the owner or manager and offer to show a 1-minute demo on your phone.
  • Referrals: ask every friend and family member: "Do you know anyone who runs a clinic, salon or shop?" A warm intro is worth ten cold messages.
  • Local WhatsApp and Facebook groups: don't advertise. Answer questions about missed appointments or customer messages helpfully, and offer a free check.
  • Your existing customers: if you already do design or video work, the same owners need this too.

Do it the right way

  • One message at a time, to the business owner. Mass-sending or buying number lists gets WhatsApp numbers banned and breaks anti-spam rules (for example, calls and SMS to numbers on a do-not-disturb list are not allowed in India).
  • Say who you are and why you're writing, keep it short, and stop if they say no.
  • Never promise results you can't prove. Offer a short free trial and measure the no-shows before and after.
  • Handle their customer data carefully: use it only to send the messages for that business, never to sell or share it.
  • Get the price and what is included in writing (WhatsApp is fine) before you start.
