Questions

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They get a normal WhatsApp message, SMS or email and reply to it.

Can I keep my own number and booking system?

Yes. We connect to what you already use, such as Google Calendar, a sheet or a booking form. If you have nothing yet, we set up a simple form for you.

Is it allowed to message my customers?

Reminders and follow-ups go only to people who booked or contacted you, with an easy way to opt out. We use approved message templates where WhatsApp requires them.

What happens to my customer data?

We keep only what's needed to send the messages (name, phone, appointment time), and we never sell or share it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How fast can it start?

Usually within 3 to 5 working days after you send the details and pay the set-up fee.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of the paid month. The set-up fee is for the work done and isn't refundable once the set-up starts.
